# Narinav

A story game where the player directs and Claude documents what happens and offers possibilities.

## Language

**Narinav**:
The game and the project. The one name for both.
_Avoid_: Navinav, Narnav, Story Buddy (the Voiceflow predecessor)

### Play

**Suggestion**:
One of the three possible next moves Claude offers the player each turn.
_Avoid_: Choice, option, prompt

**Artifact**:
The record of the story the player keeps at the end of play.
_Avoid_: Chronicle, journal, transcript

**Story bible**:
The game's running record of a story (who, where, what has happened) that lets it continue across sittings.
_Avoid_: Memory, save file, notes
